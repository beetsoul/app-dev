# Naruto

> "I'm not gonna run away, I never go back on my word! That's my nindo: my ninja way!" — Naruto Uzumaki

**Naruto** is a world-famous *action-adventure* anime series based on Masashi Kishimoto's manga. The story follows **Naruto Uzumaki**, a young ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village who dreams of becoming the **Hokage**[^1] while carrying the sealed Nine-Tailed Fox inside him.

---

## Team 7 Members

* **Naruto Uzumaki** – The energetic protagonist aiming to become Hokage.
* **Sasuke Uchiha** – Naruto's rival seeking to avenge his clan.
* **Sakura Haruno** – A skilled medical ninja with immense chakra control.
* **Kakashi Hatake** – The laid-back jonin leader known as the *Copy Ninja*.

## Top 3 Story Arcs

1. Pain's Assault Arc (*Naruto: Shippuden*)
2. Chunin Exams Arc (*Naruto*)
